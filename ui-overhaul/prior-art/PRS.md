# Open PR grading for the UI/UX overhaul: pingdotgg/t3code

- Date: 2026-10-25. Snapshot of the 2,220 open PRs from `ui-overhaul/prior-art/open-prs.md`; raw data in `/tmp/t3code-pa/`.
- Method: for each graded PR I read the body and the diff (`gh pr diff`), then ran `patch -p1 --dry-run` against our checkout (upstream `5f64c5eb`) to learn whether the diff applies as is. "Applies cleanly" means that dry run succeeded; it does not mean typecheck or tests were run. Review comments were read (`gh pr view --json comments,reviews`) for every ADOPT, every top BORROW and every overlap-bucket leader.
- Grades: ADOPT = cherry-pick with credit. BORROW = good finding, we reimplement and credit the idea. SKIP = out of scope, duplicate, superseded or not UI/UX. Default bias is BORROW or SKIP; ADOPT needs a diff that applies cleanly, is under about 150 lines, is fix-shaped, matches the DNA and has before/after evidence or a test.
- Credit format for the PR body: ADOPT is "Code from #N by `login` (title)"; BORROW is "Idea from #N by `login` (title)". Takeover PRs also name the original author where the body says so.
- Area numbers are the 24 audit areas in `ui-overhaul/audit/_AREAS.md`: 01 app-shell, 02 thread-list, 03 thread-view, 04 composer, 05 diff-and-review, 06 terminal-and-panels, 07 settings-appearance, 08 settings-project-and-keybindings, 09 providers-and-auth, 10 first-run-and-empty-states, 11 remote-access-and-pairing, 12 notifications-errors-toasts, 13 loading-and-skeletons, 14 desktop-specific, 15 mobile, 16 accessibility (also holds input-method and locale correctness), 17 typography-and-hierarchy, 18 shape-spacing-elevation, 19 motion, 20 copy-and-voice, 21 keyboard-shortcuts-ux, 22 performance-feel, 23 customisation-gap, 24 onboarding-gap.
